Designed around the window

Room to move.
Nothing to juggle.

ChainYourMac gives your windows a shared direction. The Mac menu bar app and Windows notification-area app place them side by side, then let you bring one into view at a time. No new desktop to learn. Your apps stay your apps.

01 / KEEP YOUR SIZE

Space comes from scrolling.

Opening another window adds room along the strip. Existing windows keep the dimensions you picked.

02 / STAY IN FLOW

Move with a key or click.

Open the small menu bar popover for window controls, or use global shortcuts to focus the previous or next window from anywhere.

03 / SET YOUR WIDTH

Work at your own scale.

Choose a width, center the current window, or return to the exact positions you started with.

04 / MADE FOR DESKTOP

At home in the system tray.

The Mac menu bar app and Windows notification-area app use their native window APIs to position your open app windows.

05 / ON YOUR MACHINE

Your windows stay local.

The app works directly with macOS. No account, sync service, or telemetry is part of this build.

06 / YOUR CALL

Move them back any time.

Restore the original positions in one step. Nothing is permanently rearranged.
